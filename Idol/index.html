The Extraordinary Career of Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. He built a legendary career and achieved monumental success with FC Barcelona before moving to his current Major League Soccer (MLS) club, Inter Miami CF. He has won a record 8 Ballon d'Or awards and successfully led the Argentina national team to victory in the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
